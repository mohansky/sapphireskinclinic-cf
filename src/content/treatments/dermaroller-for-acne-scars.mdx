## Microneedling: Advanced Collagen Induction Therapy

Microneedling, also known as collagen induction therapy, is a proven treatment for acne scars, stretch marks, wrinkles, and overall skin rejuvenation. Using a device with fine medical-grade needles, we create controlled micro-injuries that trigger your skin's natural healing response, producing new collagen and elastin.

### How Microneedling Works

The treatment uses a drum-shaped roller or automated pen device studded with fine microneedles (0.5-1.5mm in length). As the needles create tiny punctures in the skin:

**Healing Response Triggered** - Skin recognizes micro-injuries and begins repair
**Collagen Production** - Fibroblasts produce new collagen and elastin
**Skin Remodeling** - Over time, scars fill in and skin texture improves
**Enhanced Absorption** - Micro-channels allow better penetration of serums and treatments

### Conditions We Treat

**Acne Scars** - Rolling, boxcar, and shallow ice pick scars
**Stretch Marks** - On abdomen, thighs, arms, and breasts
**Fine Lines** - Around eyes, mouth, and forehead
**Wrinkles** - Sun damage and age-related skin aging
**Large Pores** - Reduces pore size and refines skin texture
**Uneven Tone** - Improves overall skin appearance

### The Treatment Process

1. **Consultation** - Skin assessment and treatment planning
2. **Preparation** - Skin cleansed and topical anesthetic applied
3. **Treatment** - Microneedling performed across treatment area
4. **Serum Application** - Growth factors or serums applied for enhanced absorption
5. **Aftercare** - Soothing serum and sunscreen applied

### What to Expect

**During Treatment:** Mild prickling sensation; numbing ensures comfort
**Day 1-2:** Redness and slight swelling (like a sunburn)
**Day 3-5:** Skin may feel tight or flaky as it heals
**Week 2+:** Fresh, renewed skin emerges

### Enhanced Results with PRP

Combining microneedling with PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) significantly enhances results. The micro-channels created allow PRP's growth factors to penetrate deeply, accelerating healing and collagen production.

### Treatment Protocol

**Session Frequency:** Every 4-6 weeks
**Number of Sessions:** 3-6 for optimal results
**Maintenance:** Annual treatments to maintain improvement

**Treatment time:** 30-60 minutes
**Recovery:** 2-5 days of redness
**Results:** Progressive improvement with each session
