## Q-Switched Nd:YAG Laser: Advanced Pigmentation Treatment

The Q-Switched Nd:YAG laser represents advanced technology for treating various pigmentation disorders. As a non-ablative laser, it targets pigment deep within the skin without breaking the surface, offering effective treatment with minimal downtime.

### How Q-Switched Nd:YAG Laser Works

The laser delivers light at two specific wavelengths:

**1064nm Wavelength** - Penetrates deeper into the skin, targeting:
- Deep dermal pigmentation
- Melasma
- Darker tattoo inks (black, blue)
- Safe for all skin types including darker tones

**532nm Wavelength** - Targets superficial pigmentation:
- Freckles and sun spots
- Age spots
- Red and orange tattoo inks
- Superficial birthmarks

The "Q-Switched" technology delivers ultra-short pulses (nanoseconds) of high-energy light. This energy is absorbed by melanin, causing the pigment to shatter into tiny fragments that your body naturally eliminates over time.

### Conditions We Treat

- **Melasma** - Hormonal pigmentation on face
- **Solar Lentigines** - Sun spots and age spots
- **Freckles** - Facial and body freckles
- **Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation** - Marks left by acne or injury
- **Café-au-lait Spots** - Light brown birthmarks
- **Nevus of Ota** - Bluish-grey pigmentation around eyes
- **Tattoos** - Multi-colored tattoo removal

### Treatment Benefits

- **Non-Ablative** - No breaks in skin surface
- **Minimal Downtime** - Return to activities same day
- **Safe for All Skin Types** - Including Indian and Asian skin
- **Precise Targeting** - Treats pigment without affecting surrounding skin
- **Progressive Results** - Gradual, natural-looking improvement
- **Versatile** - Treats both superficial and deep pigmentation

### The Treatment Process

1. **Assessment** - Evaluation of pigmentation type and depth
2. **Preparation** - Skin cleansed, numbing cream applied if needed
3. **Eye Protection** - Laser safety goggles placed
4. **Treatment** - Laser pulses delivered to pigmented areas
5. **Cooling** - Soothing gel applied post-treatment
6. **Aftercare** - Sun protection and care instructions

### What to Expect

**During Treatment:** Brief snapping sensation with each pulse
**Immediately After:** Temporary whitening of treated areas, mild redness
**Days Following:** Treated spots may darken before gradually lightening
**Over Weeks:** Progressive fading as body clears fragmented pigment

**Treatment time:** 15-30 minutes
**Sessions needed:** 4-8 sessions, 4-6 weeks apart
**Results timeline:** Visible improvement 2-4 weeks after each session
