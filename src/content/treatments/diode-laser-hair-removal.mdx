## Laser Hair Reduction: Permanent Solution for Unwanted Hair

Our advanced Diode Laser technology offers the gold standard in permanent hair reduction. Using the principle of selective photothermolysis, we target and destroy hair follicles while leaving surrounding skin unharmed, delivering smooth, hair-free results that last.

### How Laser Hair Removal Works

During treatment, focused light energy passes through the skin and is absorbed by melanin in the hair shaft. This absorption raises the temperature of the hair follicle, thermally destroying the cells responsible for hair regrowth. The precise wavelength, pulse duration, and power settings ensure damage to hair follicles while protecting surrounding tissue.

### The Treatment Process

1. **Consultation** - Skin assessment and treatment planning
2. **Preparation** - Area cleaned and gel applied
3. **Treatment** - Laser pulses delivered across treatment zone
4. **Cooling** - Built-in cooling for comfort
5. **Aftercare** - Soothing lotion applied

### What to Expect

**During Treatment:** Mild warmth and snapping sensation; cooling provides comfort
**Immediately After:** Slight redness that resolves within hours
**Days Following:** Treated hairs shed over 1-2 weeks
**Progressive Results:** Each session reduces hair density

### Treatment Schedule

Hair grows in cycles, and laser only affects actively growing hair. Multiple sessions ensure all follicles are treated:

- **Face:** 4-6 weeks between sessions
- **Body:** 6-8 weeks between sessions
- **Total Sessions:** 6-8 for optimal results

**Treatment time:** 15-60 minutes depending on area
**Sessions needed:** 6-8 treatments
**Maintenance:** Occasional touch-ups as needed
