## Nail Surgery: Permanent Solutions for Nail Problems

Nail surgery is a specialized dermatological procedure designed to address persistent nail conditions that don't respond to conservative treatments. Our experienced dermatologists perform minimally invasive nail surgeries under local anesthesia, providing permanent relief from painful and recurring nail problems.

### Conditions We Treat

**Ingrown Toenails (Onychocryptosis)**
- Nails growing into surrounding skin
- Recurring infections and inflammation
- Chronic pain and tenderness

**Nail Infections**
- Severe fungal nail infections (onychomycosis)
- Bacterial nail bed infections
- Chronic paronychia

**Nail Deformities**
- Thickened nails (onychogryphosis)
- Curved or pincer nails
- Traumatic nail damage

### Types of Nail Surgery

**Partial Nail Avulsion** - Removal of the problematic portion of the nail, usually the ingrown edge. The nail matrix may be treated to prevent regrowth of the troublesome section.

**Total Nail Avulsion** - Complete removal of the nail plate when the entire nail is affected. The nail bed is preserved for healthy regrowth.

**Nail Matrixectomy** - Permanent destruction of part or all of the nail matrix to prevent regrowth. Options include:
- Chemical matrixectomy (phenol application)
- Surgical matrixectomy

### The Nail Surgery Process

1. **Assessment** - Evaluation of the nail condition and medical history
2. **Preparation** - Cleaning and sterilization of the surgical area
3. **Anesthesia** - Local anesthetic injection for complete numbness
4. **Procedure** - Careful removal of affected nail tissue
5. **Treatment** - Application of chemical or surgical matrixectomy if needed
6. **Dressing** - Sterile bandaging with aftercare instructions

### What to Expect

**During Surgery:** The toe is completely numb; you may feel pressure but no pain.
**First Week:** Keep the foot elevated when possible. Mild discomfort is normal and manageable.
**Healing Period:** 4-8 weeks for complete healing. The nail bed gradually heals beneath the dressing.
**Long-term:** Permanent resolution of the problem with proper nail regrowth (if applicable).

### Post-Operative Care

- Keep the dressing clean and dry for the first 24-48 hours
- Soak in warm salt water as directed after initial healing
- Wear comfortable, open-toed footwear when possible
- Attend scheduled follow-up appointments
- Take prescribed antibiotics and pain relief as needed
