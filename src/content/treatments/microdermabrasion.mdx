## Microdermabrasion: Crystal-Free Diamond Peel for Radiant Skin

Microdermabrasion with OxyPeel is a non-surgical method of promoting skin renewal through an amazing crystal-free diamond peel method. This gentle yet effective treatment deeply exfoliates your skin, removing dead cells and rejuvenating your complexion for a fresher, more youthful appearance.

### How Microdermabrasion Works

Our advanced diamond peel system uses a diamond-tipped wand to gently buff away the outer layer of dead skin cells while simultaneously vacuuming away debris. Combined with our OxyPeel system, the treatment:

**Deep Exfoliation** - Removes dead skin cells that dull your complexion
**Pore Cleansing** - Unclogs pores and reduces blackheads
**Circulation Boost** - Stimulates blood flow for healthy skin function
**Product Penetration** - Enhances absorption of skincare products
**Oxygen Infusion** - Delivers revitalizing oxygen for fresh, glowing skin

### Ideal Candidates

Microdermabrasion is suitable for most skin types and addresses:
- Dull, lackluster complexion
- Fine lines and early wrinkles
- Enlarged pores and blackheads
- Minor acne scarring
- Uneven skin texture
- Sun-damaged skin

### The Treatment Process

1. **Cleansing** - Skin thoroughly cleaned
2. **Diamond Peel** - Gentle exfoliation with vacuum suction
3. **OxyPeel** - Chemical oxy treatment for deeper cleansing
4. **Oxygen Infusion** - Revitalizing oxygen mist application
5. **Hydration** - Moisturizer and sunscreen applied

**During Treatment:** Mild scratching sensation; most find it relaxing
**Immediately After:** Fresh, glowing complexion; slight pinkness possible
**Following Days:** Smoother, brighter skin
**Over Time:** Progressive improvement with regular treatments

**Treatment time:** 30-45 minutes
**Recommended series:** 6-10 treatments every 2-4 weeks
**Maintenance:** Monthly sessions
