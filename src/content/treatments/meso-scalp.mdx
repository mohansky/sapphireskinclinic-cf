## Mesotherapy for Scalp: Nourish Your Hair from Within

Hair mesotherapy is a specialized treatment that delivers essential vitamins, minerals, and amino acids directly into your scalp through micro-injections. This targeted approach nourishes hair follicles at the root level, promoting healthier, stronger hair growth while reducing hair fall.

### How Scalp Mesotherapy Works

Our hair requires specific nutrients to maintain healthy growth cycles. Environmental factors, stress, hormonal changes, and nutritional deficiencies can all impact hair health. Mesotherapy addresses these issues by:

**Direct Nutrient Delivery** - Bypasses the digestive system to deliver nutrients exactly where needed

**Improved Blood Circulation** - Enhances microcirculation in the scalp for better nutrient absorption

**Follicle Stimulation** - Awakens dormant hair follicles and strengthens existing ones

**DHT Blocking** - Certain formulations help counteract the hormone responsible for pattern baldness

### Treatment Benefits

- **Reduced Hair Fall** - Noticeable decrease in hair shedding
- **Increased Hair Density** - More hair per square centimeter
- **Improved Hair Quality** - Stronger, shinier, healthier hair
- **Scalp Health** - Balanced scalp environment for optimal growth
- **No Downtime** - Return to activities immediately
- **Customizable** - Formulations tailored to your specific needs

### What's in the Mesotherapy Cocktail?

Our customized formulations may include:
- **B-Complex Vitamins** - Essential for hair growth and strength
- **Biotin** - Promotes keratin production
- **Amino Acids** - Building blocks for hair protein
- **Minerals** - Zinc, copper, and iron for healthy follicles
- **Growth Factors** - Stimulate cellular regeneration
- **Hyaluronic Acid** - Hydrates and nourishes the scalp

### The Treatment Process

1. **Assessment** - Scalp analysis and hair health evaluation
2. **Preparation** - Scalp cleansed and numbed if needed
3. **Injection** - Micro-injections across the treatment area
4. **Massage** - Gentle massage to distribute the solution
5. **Aftercare** - Home care instructions provided

### What to Expect

**During Treatment:** Mild pinprick sensations; generally well-tolerated
**Immediately After:** Slight redness that resolves within hours
**First Few Sessions:** Reduced hair shedding
**Over Time:** Progressive improvement in hair thickness and quality

**Treatment time:** 30-45 minutes
**Recommended course:** 4-6 sessions, 1-2 weeks apart
**Maintenance:** Monthly sessions as needed
