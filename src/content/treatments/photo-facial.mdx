## Photo Facial: Light-Based Skin Rejuvenation

Photo facial treatments harness the power of light to address various skin concerns, from sun damage and pigmentation to fine lines and acne. Our clinic offers both LED and IPL technologies, allowing us to customize treatment to your specific skin needs and goals.

### Understanding Photo Facial Technologies

**LED Photo Facials** use narrow-spectrum light at specific wavelengths:
- **Red Light** - Stimulates collagen production for anti-aging benefits
- **Blue Light** - Kills acne-causing bacteria
- **Combination** - Addresses multiple concerns simultaneously

**IPL Photo Facials** deliver intense pulsed light to target:
- **Pigmentation** - Brown spots and sun damage
- **Vascular Issues** - Broken capillaries and redness
- **Texture** - Stimulates collagen remodeling

### What Photo Facials Treat

- **Sun Damage** - Age spots, freckles, and sun spots
- **Vascular Concerns** - Broken capillaries, spider veins, rosacea
- **Skin Tone** - Uneven pigmentation and discoloration
- **Aging Signs** - Fine lines and loss of elasticity
- **Acne** - Active breakouts and post-acne redness
- **Skin Texture** - Enlarged pores and rough texture

### Treatment Benefits

- **Non-Invasive** - No needles, incisions, or injections
- **Minimal Downtime** - Return to activities same day
- **Progressive Results** - Improvement builds with each session
- **Customizable** - Tailored to your specific concerns
- **Safe** - Clinically proven technology
- **Comfortable** - Especially with LED treatments

### The Treatment Process

1. **Consultation** - Skin analysis and treatment planning
2. **Cleansing** - Skin thoroughly cleaned
3. **Eye Protection** - Goggles applied for safety
4. **Treatment** - Light pulses delivered across treatment area
5. **Cooling** - Soothing gel or cooling applied (IPL)
6. **Aftercare** - Sunscreen and moisturizer applied

### What to Expect

**LED Photo Facial:**
- Completely painless and relaxing
- No redness or downtime
- Gradual improvement over series

**IPL Photo Facial:**
- Mild snapping sensation
- Temporary redness for a few hours
- Pigmented spots may darken temporarily before flaking

**Treatment time:** 30-60 minutes
**Recommended course:** 4-6 sessions, 2-4 weeks apart
**Maintenance:** Monthly treatments as needed
