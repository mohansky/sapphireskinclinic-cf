## Skin Tag & Wart Removal: Quick, Safe, and Effective Treatment

Skin tags, warts, and other benign skin growths are extremely common and often develop in areas of friction or sun exposure. While harmless, these growths can be cosmetically bothersome or cause irritation. At Sapphire Skin Clinic, we offer multiple advanced techniques for quick and effective removal with minimal downtime.

<br/>
### Conditions We Treat

**Skin Tags (Acrochordons)**
Small, soft, flesh-colored growths that typically appear on the neck, underarms, eyelids, and body folds. They're harmless but can become irritated by clothing or jewelry.

**Warts**
- **Common Warts** - Rough, raised growths usually on hands and fingers
- **Flat Warts** - Smooth, flat-topped lesions often on face and legs
- **Plantar Warts** - Growths on the soles of feet
- **Filiform Warts** - Thread-like warts around the face

**Other Benign Growths**
- Seborrheic keratoses (age spots)
- Dermatosis papulosa nigra (small dark bumps)
- Milia (tiny white cysts)
- Cherry angiomas (red spots)

### Our Removal Techniques

**Electrocautery**
Uses controlled electrical current to remove growths precisely. Ideal for skin tags and small lesions. Minimal bleeding and quick healing.

**Radiofrequency (RF) Removal**
Advanced technology using radio waves for precise tissue removal with minimal thermal damage to surrounding skin. Excellent for facial lesions.

**Cryotherapy**
Freezing technique using liquid nitrogen to destroy unwanted tissue. Effective for warts and seborrheic keratoses.

**Laser Therapy**
Targeted light energy for precise removal of superficial growths with minimal scarring. Best for flat lesions and sensitive areas.

### The Treatment Process

1. **Consultation** - Assessment of growths and selection of appropriate technique
2. **Preparation** - Cleaning the area and applying anesthesia if needed
3. **Treatment** - Precise removal using the chosen method
4. **Aftercare** - Application of soothing cream and wound care instructions

### What to Expect

**During Treatment:** Quick and minimally uncomfortable. Most procedures take just seconds per lesion.

**Immediately After:** Slight redness or minor scabbing at the treatment site.

**Healing Period:** 3-7 days for the treated area to heal completely.

**Final Results:** Clear, smooth skin with minimal to no visible scarring.

### Aftercare Instructions

- Keep the treated area clean and dry
- Apply antibiotic ointment as directed
- Avoid picking at scabs or crusts
- Protect from sun exposure during healing
- Follow specific instructions based on treatment type
