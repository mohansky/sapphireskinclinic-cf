## Tattoo Removal: Advanced Laser Technology for Clear Skin

Tattoo removal has evolved significantly, and our Q-switched Nd:YAG laser technology offers safe, effective treatment to fade or completely remove unwanted tattoos. Whether your tattoo no longer fits your image or you simply want a fresh start, we can help you achieve clear skin with minimal scarring.

### How Laser Tattoo Removal Works

Our advanced Q-switched Nd:YAG laser delivers ultra-short pulses of light energy that pass through the skin and are absorbed by tattoo ink particles. This energy breaks down the ink into microscopic fragments that your body's immune system gradually eliminates through natural processes.

**Selective Targeting** - Laser wavelengths specifically target ink pigments
**Ink Fragmentation** - High-energy pulses shatter ink particles
**Natural Elimination** - Body's lymphatic system clears fragmented ink
**Progressive Fading** - Each session lightens the tattoo further

### What We Can Treat

- **Professional Tattoos** - Multi-colored, deeply placed ink
- **Amateur Tattoos** - Homemade or hand-poked tattoos
- **Cosmetic Tattoos** - Permanent makeup, microblading
- **Partial Removal** - Lightening for cover-up tattoos
- **Various Colors** - Black, blue, red, green, and more

### Treatment Process

1. **Consultation** - Assessment of tattoo type, size, colors, and skin type
2. **Preparation** - Area cleaned and protective eyewear applied
3. **Treatment** - Laser pulses delivered across tattoo
4. **Cooling** - Ice or cooling applied for comfort
5. **Aftercare** - Bandaging and detailed care instructions

### Factors Affecting Treatment

**Number of Sessions Needed:**
- Tattoo age and quality
- Ink colors used
- Depth of ink placement
- Location on body
- Your immune system response
- Amateur vs. professional application

### What to Expect

**During Treatment:** Sensation similar to rubber band snapping; cooling helps comfort
**After Treatment:** Whitening, redness, and possible blistering (normal healing response)
**Days Following:** Scabbing and gradual healing over 1-2 weeks
**Between Sessions:** 6-8 weeks to allow complete healing and ink clearance
