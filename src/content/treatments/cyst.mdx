## Cyst Removal: Safe and Effective Treatment for Skin Cysts

Skin cysts are common, benign growths that develop beneath the skin's surface. While usually harmless, they can become uncomfortable, unsightly, or prone to infection. Our dermatologists specialize in the safe removal of various types of skin cysts with minimal scarring.

### Types of Skin Cysts We Treat

**Epidermoid Cysts** - The most common type, filled with keratin and skin cells, typically appearing on the face, neck, or trunk

**Sebaceous Cysts** - Form when sebaceous glands become blocked, often found on the scalp, face, and back

**Pilar Cysts** - Smooth, firm cysts that develop around hair follicles, commonly on the scalp

**Milia** - Tiny, white cysts that appear when dead skin becomes trapped beneath the surface

### When to Consider Cyst Removal

- The cyst is growing in size
- It becomes painful, inflamed, or infected
- It's located in a visible or cosmetically concerning area
- It interferes with daily activities or clothing
- You want definitive treatment to prevent recurrence

### Our Removal Techniques

**Surgical Excision** - Complete removal of the cyst and its capsule to prevent recurrence

**Minimal Incision Extraction** - Smaller incision technique for appropriate cysts with reduced scarring

**Drainage and Injection** - For inflamed cysts, followed by steroid injection to reduce swelling

### What to Expect

**Procedure Duration:** 15-30 minutes depending on cyst size and location
**Anesthesia:** Local anesthesia for a pain-free experience
**Recovery:** Most patients return to normal activities within 1-2 days
**Follow-up:** Suture removal after 7-14 days if required

After removal, tissue is sent for pathological examination to confirm the diagnosis and rule out any concerns. Our goal is complete removal with optimal cosmetic outcomes.
