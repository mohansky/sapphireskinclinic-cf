## PRP for Hair: Natural Hair Restoration Therapy

Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy is a state-of-the-art, non-surgical treatment for hair loss that harnesses your body's own healing power. By concentrating the growth factors in your blood, we stimulate natural hair growth and restore thickness to thinning hair.

### How PRP Hair Treatment Works

A small sample of your blood is drawn and processed in a centrifuge to separate and concentrate the platelets. This platelet-rich plasma contains powerful growth factors that, when injected into the scalp, stimulate dormant hair follicles and promote healthier, thicker hair growth.

**The Process:**
1. Blood is drawn from your arm (similar to a routine blood test)
2. The sample is processed to concentrate platelets (4x normal levels)
3. The activated PRP is injected into thinning areas of the scalp
4. Growth factors stimulate follicles and promote new hair growth

### Benefits of PRP Hair Therapy

- **Non-Surgical** - No incisions, scars, or lengthy recovery
- **Natural Results** - Uses your own blood, minimizing allergy risk
- **Stimulates Growth** - Activates dormant follicles
- **Improves Hair Quality** - Thicker, stronger hair strands
- **Quick Procedure** - Completed in 60-90 minutes
- **Minimal Downtime** - Return to activities same day

### Who Is a Good Candidate?

PRP hair therapy is suitable for both men and women experiencing:
- Early-stage hair thinning
- Androgenetic alopecia (pattern hair loss)
- Thinning hair that hasn't responded to other treatments
- Those seeking to complement hair transplant results
- Patients wanting preventive hair loss treatment

### Treatment Protocol

**Initial Phase:** 3-4 treatments spaced 4-6 weeks apart
**Maintenance:** Sessions every 6-12 months
**Combination Therapy:** Often paired with hair transplants or medications for enhanced results

### Results Timeline

**Month 1-2:** Reduced hair shedding
**Month 3-4:** Early signs of new hair growth
**Month 6-12:** Visible improvement in hair density and thickness

**Treatment time:** 60-90 minutes
**Sessions needed:** 3-4 initial, ongoing maintenance
**Recovery:** Resume normal activities immediately
