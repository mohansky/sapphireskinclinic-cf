## Chemical Peels: Reveal Smoother, Brighter Skin

Chemical peels are a proven skin resurfacing treatment that removes damaged outer layers to reveal healthier, more youthful skin beneath. By applying a specially formulated chemical solution, we can address a wide range of skin concerns with minimal downtime.

### How Chemical Peels Work

A chemical solution is applied to the skin, causing controlled exfoliation. As the old skin peels away over the following days, new skin emerges that is typically smoother, less wrinkled, and more evenly toned.

### Types of Chemical Peels

**Light (Superficial) Peels**
- Uses mild acids like alpha-hydroxy acid (AHA)
- Treats fine wrinkles, uneven skin tone, and dryness
- Minimal recovery time (1-3 days)
- Can be done monthly

**Medium Peels**
- Uses trichloroacetic acid (TCA) or glycolic acid
- Treats moderate wrinkles, acne scars, and pigmentation
- Recovery time of 5-7 days
- Recommended every 3-6 months

**Deep Peels**
- Uses phenol or high-concentration TCA
- Treats deep wrinkles, severe sun damage, and precancerous growths
- Requires 10-14 days recovery
- Dramatic, long-lasting results

### Conditions We Treat

Chemical peels can effectively address:
- Fine lines under the eyes and around the mouth
- Wrinkles caused by sun damage and aging
- Mild to moderate acne scars
- Active acne and enlarged pores
- Age spots, freckles, and dark patches (melasma)
- Rough skin texture
- Dull, uneven skin tone

### The Treatment Process

1. **Consultation** - Skin assessment and peel selection
2. **Preparation** - Cleansing and possible pre-treatment regimen
3. **Application** - Chemical solution applied in controlled layers
4. **Neutralization** - Solution removed or neutralized
5. **Aftercare** - Soothing products and sun protection applied

### What to Expect

**During Treatment:** Tingling or mild burning sensation that subsides quickly
**Days 1-3:** Skin may feel tight, with redness and initial peeling
**Days 4-7:** Peeling continues; new skin emerges
**Week 2+:** Fresh, rejuvenated skin revealed

**Treatment time:** 30-60 minutes
**Recovery:** 3-14 days depending on peel depth
**Results:** Progressive improvement with each treatment
