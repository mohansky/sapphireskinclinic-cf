## What Is PRX Plus (PRX-T33)?

PRX Plus is a medical-grade biorevitalization treatment developed in Italy. It's based on a unique formula containing three key active ingredients:

- **TCA (Trichloroacetic Acid) 33%**: A powerful chemical exfoliant that stimulates deep dermal regeneration — but in this formula, it works without causing surface peeling
- **Low-concentration Hydrogen Peroxide (H2O2)**: Acts as a protective shield, preventing the TCA from damaging the outer skin layer while allowing it to penetrate and stimulate the deeper layers
- **Kojic Acid 5%**: A natural brightening agent that helps reduce pigmentation and even out skin tone

This patented combination is what makes PRX Plus unique — it delivers the regenerative power of a medium-depth peel while keeping the skin's surface intact.

## How Does PRX Plus Work?

Traditional chemical peels work by removing the outer layers of skin, causing visible peeling and requiring days of recovery. PRX Plus takes a fundamentally different approach:

1. **Deep stimulation without surface damage**: The hydrogen peroxide neutralizes the TCA's exfoliating effect on the epidermis while allowing it to stimulate fibroblasts in the deeper dermis
2. **Collagen and elastin activation**: TCA triggers a controlled inflammatory response in the dermis, activating fibroblasts to produce new collagen and elastin
3. **Glycosaminoglycan production**: The treatment stimulates production of hyaluronic acid and other molecules that keep skin plump and hydrated
4. **Brightening effect**: Kojic acid works on melanocytes to reduce excess pigmentation

The result? Your skin gets the deep regenerative signal without the surface trauma.

One of PRX Plus's unique advantages is that it's safe and effective for all skin types, including darker skin tones that may not be suitable candidates for certain laser treatments or traditional peels.

## What to Expect During Treatment

A PRX Plus session is quick, comfortable, and fits easily into a busy schedule:

1. **Cleansing**: The skin is thoroughly cleansed and degreased
2. **Application**: The PRX Plus solution is massaged into the skin using a specific technique — typically 3–5 passes depending on the treatment area and skin response
3. **Sensation**: You'll feel a warm, tingling sensation during application — this is normal and indicates the product is working
4. **Neutralization**: The skin is cleansed and a neutralizing cream is applied
5. **Post-treatment care**: A hydrating and nourishing cream is applied
6. **Duration**: The entire session takes just 15–30 minutes

There is no visible peeling, no frosting, and no significant redness. Most patients return to their normal activities immediately after treatment.

## How Many Sessions Are Needed?

The recommended treatment protocol depends on your concerns:

- **Rejuvenation and glow**: 3–4 sessions, spaced 7–10 days apart
- **Anti-aging and firming**: 4–6 sessions, spaced 7–14 days apart
- **Scar treatment**: 5–8 sessions, spaced 14 days apart
- **Stretch marks**: 5–8 sessions, spaced 14 days apart

Maintenance sessions every 1–3 months help sustain results long-term.

## Who Should Avoid PRX Plus?

While PRX Plus is safe for most people, it's not recommended for:

- Pregnant or breastfeeding women
- Active skin infections or open wounds in the treatment area
- Known allergy to any of the ingredients
- Very sensitive or compromised skin barrier (treatment may be postponed until skin health improves)
